Our Services

Fuji Flame is a live hibachi catering service — we don't have a restaurant or sushi counter. Our chef cooks teppanyaki-style, right in front of your guests, wherever your event is happening.

What's Included

  • A live hibachi chef who cooks your meal teppanyaki-style, right at your event
  • A full show along with the meal — flaming onion volcanoes, knife tricks and more
  • Your choice of one of five catering packages, priced per guest
  • Japanese Fried Rice and Hibachi Vegetables included with every package
  • Our three signature house sauces: Yum Yum, Ginger and Fuji Spicy
  • Catering for events such as birthdays, weddings and corporate parties
